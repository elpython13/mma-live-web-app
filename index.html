What's in the app

  • Results from promotions worldwide

    Fight cards and results from UFC, ONE, PFL, RIZIN, Cage Warriors and more, by day. Pick a date to see what happened or what's coming up.

  • Fighter profiles and records

    Each fighter's win–loss–draw record, career stats and full fight history. Browse fighters A–Z or by division, with champions marked.

  • Events and the week ahead

    A week-ahead overview of upcoming events, full event cards with each bout, and a news feed from MMA news sources.

  • Your Following list

    Follow the fighters and promotions you care about and find them in one tab. Your list stays on your device, with no sign-up.

  • Search the whole app

    Find fighters, events, promotions, divisions and news from a single search bar.
